# 01 — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
# SANCHAY — Unified Government Digital Service Platform

**Product:** Sanchay (संचय)  
**Meaning:** Accumulation / collection  
**Purpose:** Unified citizen-facing access layer for government services  
**SIH MVP:** NTA / JEE Main + Ayushman Bharat  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Foundation Specification

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Sanchay is a **unified citizen-facing government service platform** that brings fragmented government services into one consistent digital experience.

Citizens should not have to understand which department owns a service, discover a separate portal, create multiple accounts, repeatedly enter the same information, or interpret complicated government instructions.

Sanchay organizes services through a common hierarchy:

```text
Sanchay
  ↓
Department / Domain
  ↓
Organization / Government Service Provider
  ↓
Service
  ↓
Workflow
```

Example:

```text
Sanchay
  ↓
Education
  ↓
NTA
  ↓
JEE Main
  ↓
Application
```

and:

```text
Sanchay
  ↓
Healthcare
  ↓
Ayushman Bharat
  ↓
Eligibility / Card / Services
```

Where authorized integrations are available, the citizen experiences these services as parts of Sanchay even when official government infrastructure remains underneath.

Sanchay supports two complementary interaction modes:

1. **Traditional interface** — browse, search, open services, complete workflows, track applications.
2. **Contextual AI interface** — a persistent Sanchay AI assistant that understands the citizen's current department, service, page, workflow, and authorized profile context.

The AI is not the product by itself. **The unified government-service experience is the product; AI is a contextual layer that reduces friction.**

---

# 2. Problem Statement

> **Government services are fragmented across multiple portals, forcing citizens to repeatedly navigate, authenticate, enter data, and understand complex processes instead of accessing services through one simple, unified, citizen-centric platform.**

---

# 3. Vision

> **One citizen. One platform. Many government services.**

Sanchay aims to become a common citizen-facing layer through which a person can discover, understand, and complete government services without needing to learn the structure of government websites.

The long-term vision is not necessarily to remove underlying government systems. Instead, Sanchay aims to remove **citizen-facing fragmentation** while preserving official authority, security, existing infrastructure, and authorized government integrations.

---

# 4. Product Principles

## 4.1 Citizen-first

The system is organized around what citizens want to accomplish rather than how government departments are internally structured.

## 4.2 Traditional + AI

AI is optional. Every supported service should remain usable through a conventional interface.

## 4.3 Context-aware AI

Sanchay AI should know where the citizen is and what they are doing.

```text
Current user
   +
Current department
   +
Current organization
   +
Current service
   +
Current page
   +
Current workflow
   +
Authorized profile context
   =
Sanchay AI context
```

## 4.4 Official-source grounded

Government information should be retrieved from authoritative sources and presented with source attribution where appropriate.

## 4.5 One platform account

A verified citizen should have one Sanchay platform account/UID. Aadhaar, mobile number, email, or another approved identity mechanism may be used for authentication/verification, but none is the platform UID itself.

## 4.6 Consent and minimum data

Only required and authorized information should be used or shared.

## 4.7 Human control

AI can guide, prepare, recommend, and automate permitted steps, but consequential actions require appropriate citizen confirmation.

## 4.8 Modular services

NTA and Ayushman are initial services, not hardcoded exceptions. New services should be incorporable through standardized service integrations.

# 9. Contextual Sanchay AI

A small persistent AI entry point should be available throughout the platform.

Example:

```text
                         ◯
                        AI
```

When opened, it should transition to a familiar conversational interface inspired by modern chat applications while retaining Sanchay branding and government-service context.

### Context example

If the user is on:

```text
Education
  → NTA
    → JEE Main
      → Application
        → Personal Information
```

and asks:

> "What is this asking for?"

Sanchay AI should understand that "this" refers to the current application context.

The user should not have to repeat the service name every time.

# 10. AI Capabilities

Sanchay AI should be capable of:

- Understanding natural-language intent
- Discovering services
- Explaining government information
- Retrieving official sources through RAG
- Checking supported eligibility conditions
- Reading authorized profile information
- Identifying missing information
- Pre-filling supported fields
- Asking only necessary questions

- Preparing applications
- Providing official portal access
- Tracking supported applications
- Escalating to traditional workflows when appropriate

# 11. Example: JEE Workflow

### Ineligible citizen

```text
User:
"Help me apply for JEE."

Sanchay:
1. Identify JEE Main
2. Retrieve current requirements
3. Check supported eligibility conditions
4. Identify that the user cannot proceed
5. Explain the relevant requirement
6. Provide the official source
```

The system should avoid making the citizen complete unnecessary application steps.

### Eligible citizen

```text
User:
"Help me apply for JEE."

Sanchay:
1. Identify JEE Main
2. Check eligibility
3. Load authorized profile data
4. Detect available documents
5. Pre-fill supported fields
6. Identify missing information
7. Ask for user decisions
8. Recommend available choices where appropriate
9. Present application review
10. Obtain confirmation
11. Perform only authorized final actions
```

# 13. Unified Citizen Account

Sanchay should provide one platform account associated with a unique internal UID.

The identity model is:

```text
Approved identity verification
            ↓
     Existing account?
       ↙          ↘
     YES           NO
      ↓             ↓
Existing UID    Create UID
```

The UID should be:

- Platform-generated
- Random/non-meaningful
- Stable
- Unique
- Independent of Aadhaar
- Independent of phone number
- Independent of email

Multiple approved authentication methods may be linked to the same account after appropriate verification.

---

# 14. Citizen Profile

The profile is a reusable information layer for supported government workflows.

Possible categories:

- Basic information
- Date of birth
- Contact information
- Address
- Education
- Service-specific information
- Authorized documents
- Consent/data-sharing preferences

The system must not assume that every service can access every field.

---

# 15. Automatic Data Reuse

Sanchay should eliminate unnecessary repeated entry.

```text
Citizen Profile
      ↓
Service field mapping
      ↓
Application
```

Example:

```text
Profile: full_name
        ↓
NTA: candidate_name

Profile: full_name
        ↓
Ayushman: beneficiary_name
```

The user should be able to review populated information before consequential submission.

---

# 16. RAG / Official Knowledge Layer

Sanchay AI requires a Retrieval-Augmented Generation system.

```text
Official Sources
      ↓
Ingestion
      ↓
Extraction / Cleaning
      ↓
Chunking
      ↓
Embeddings
      ↓
Vector Database
      ↓
Retrieval
      ↓
Reranking
      ↓
LLM
      ↓
Answer + Source
```

The knowledge layer should support:

- Official webpages
- PDFs
- Bulletins
- Notifications
- Circulars
- FAQs
- Guidelines
- Rules
- Other approved government information

Knowledge records should retain source metadata and freshness/version information where available.

---

# 17. Existing Government Infrastructure

Sanchay should be compatible with existing government infrastructure.

Where authorized, integrations may support:

- Official APIs
- Government SSO
- Deep links
- Authorized data exchange
- Application status
- Official payment mechanisms
- Other approved service actions

Where direct integration is unavailable, Sanchay should provide the official portal rather than pretending an action was completed.

Sanchay must never bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, authorization, rate limits, or other government security controls.

# 24. Service Capability Model

Every integrated government service SHALL be modeled as a set of capabilities that Sanchay can expose through both traditional UI and Sanchay AI.

```text
SERVICE
│
├── Knowledge
│   └── Official information AI can retrieve/explain
│
├── Citizen Data
│   └── Authorized user/service information AI can access
│
├── Documents
│   └── Documents AI can retrieve, explain, prepare, or process
│
├── Actions
│   └── Authorized operations AI can initiate/perform
│
└── Status
    └── Official state/results AI can retrieve
```

This prevents Sanchay from being designed around individual examples such as JEE or Ayushman.

JEE is an implementation of the generic service model, not a special case.

---

# 25. Sanchay AI Is a Service Interface

Sanchay AI is not limited to answering questions.

For an integrated service, AI may provide four categories of capability:

### Knowledge

```text
"What is the JEE eligibility?"
"When is the answer key released?"
"What does this rule mean?"
```

Powered primarily by the official knowledge/RAG layer.

### Retrieval

```text
"Show my admit card."
"Show my result."
"Show my application status."
"Get my answer key."
```

Powered by authorized service data sources.

### Actions

```text
"Start my application."
"Fill my application."
"Update this permitted field."
"Submit after I confirm."
```

Powered by authorized service tools/APIs.

### Transformation / Assistance

```text
"Explain question 17."
"Summarize this notification."
"Tell me what information is missing."
"Compare these available options."
```

Powered by retrieved official/service information plus AI reasoning.

The AI SHALL only perform capabilities that the underlying service exposes and Sanchay is authorized to use.

---

# 27. Product Boundary

Sanchay SHALL NOT assume that every service can support every capability.

For each service:

```text
Available capability
        ↓
Authorized integration?
        ↓
Yes → Sanchay can expose it
No  → Provide information / official portal / safe fallback
```

Sanchay SHALL never pretend an unsupported action exists.
